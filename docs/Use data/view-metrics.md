## Metadata

After the processor is successfully uploaded, you can first check the status of the processor on [**Datasource**](doc:the-monitoring) tab. Make sure the status is shown as <span style={{ color: "green" }}>**PROCESSING**</span>. Also, take notice of the **block number** or **Version** on each chain (Yes, we support multi-chain: [multi-chain-support](doc:multi-chain)) is processed at.

<Image align="center" border={false} caption="Viewing Data Source" src="https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/sentioxyz/docs/HEAD/assets/datasource.gif" />

You can also examine the status of the collected [metrics](doc:metrics) on the Metrics tab.

<Image align="center" border={false} caption="Viewing Metrics" src="https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/sentioxyz/docs/HEAD/assets/image%20(5)%20(4)%20(1).png" />

Clicking on each [metric](doc:metrics) gives you a more detailed view of the metric, including tags and visualization of the metrics.
